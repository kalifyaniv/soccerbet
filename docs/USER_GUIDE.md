# 📖 Participant Guide — World Cup 2026 Betting Pool

Welcome to our World Cup 2026 group-stage betting competition!  
This guide covers everything you need — from signing up before the tournament to watching your score climb once the games begin.

---

## The basics

You predict the **exact final score** of all 72 group-stage matches **before the tournament starts**.  
Points are awarded automatically as the admin enters each result. The player with the most points wins.

### Point table

| Points | When you earn them |
|--------|-------------------|
| **7** | Exact score (e.g. you predicted 2–1, final was 2–1) |
| **4** | Correct winner **and** correct goal margin (e.g. predicted 3–1, final was 2–0 — both a 1-goal win) |
| **3** | Correct winner **or** correct goal margin (only one of the two) |
| **0** | Wrong result |
| **×2** | Any match that you (or anyone) marked as a multiplier — points doubled |
| **+10** | You predicted the tournament's top scorer correctly |
| **+10** | You predicted the tournament winner correctly |

### Multiplier matches

When you submit your bets, you choose **2 matches** to mark as multipliers (⭐).  
All players' multiplier picks are combined into one shared pool — every match someone picked becomes a multiplier **for everyone**.  
This means the more players pick different matches, the more multiplier matches exist.

## Before the tournament — what to do

### 1 · Register

1. Open the app and click **הרשמה** (Register)
2. Enter your name, email address, and a password
3. You're in — the entry fee (₪200) is handled separately with the organizer

### 2 · Fill in your 72 predictions

1. From the menu, go to **הימורים** (Bets)
2. You'll see tabs for each group: **A, B, C … L** — 12 groups, 6 matches each
3. For every match, enter the score you predict:
   - Left number = goals for the **home** team (listed first)
   - Right number = goals for the **away** team (listed second)
4. Mark exactly **2 matches** with the ⭐ star icon as your multiplier picks
5. Fill in **King of Goals** (top scorer prediction) and **Tournament Winner** at the bottom of the form

> **Tip:** The form auto-saves a draft every 30 seconds — you won't lose your work if you close the browser. You can also come back and edit your predictions as many times as you like until the deadline.

### 3 · Preview and submit

1. Once all 72 matches are filled and 2 multipliers are selected, the **Submit** button activates
2. Click it — a preview modal appears showing everything you've predicted
3. Tick the confirmation checkbox and click **אישור** (Confirm)

> ⚠️ **Submission is permanent and irreversible.** Once confirmed, your predictions are locked and cannot be changed. Double-check before you confirm.

### 4 · Deadline

The deadline is **before the first match kicks off** on 11 June 2026.  
A countdown timer on the betting page shows exactly how much time remains.  
After the deadline, the form becomes read-only even if you haven't submitted — so submit early.

## During the tournament — how to follow along

### Leaderboard

Go to **לוח התוצאות** (Leaderboard) to see:

- **Overall standings** — ranked by total points, updated after every match result is entered
- **Group leaders** (מובילי הבתים) — who's leading in each of the 12 World Cup groups (A–L)

The leaderboard refreshes automatically — just reload the page after a result is announced.

### Results page

Go to **תוצאות** (Results) to see:

- All 72 matches, split by group
- Each match shows: your prediction · actual score · points you earned · scoring method (exact / winner+gap / winner or gap / wrong)
- Your running group-stage total and bonus points

### When are points updated?

Points appear after the admin manually enters each final score. This typically happens within a few hours of each match ending. Bonus points (King of Goals, Tournament Winner) are added at the end of the tournament.

## FAQ

**Can I change my predictions after submitting?**  
No. Once you click Confirm, predictions are locked permanently.

**What if I save a draft but never submit?**  
Your draft is saved but you get 0 points for every match — only locked bets count.

**What if I forget the deadline?**  
After the deadline the form locks automatically. Submit early to be safe.

**How do I know my bet was locked?**  
The betting page switches to a read-only view with a "🔒 ההימורים שלך נעולים" message at the top.

**Is a 0–0 draw a valid prediction?**  
Yes, any score from 0–0 upward is valid.

**What counts as the "goal margin"?**  
The absolute difference between the two scores. A 3–1 win and a 2–0 win both have a margin of 2 — so predicting 3–1 against a 2–0 result earns 4 points (correct winner + correct gap), not 7.

**What does ⭐ multiplier mean exactly?**  
Points for that match are doubled. If you'd normally earn 7 pts on an exact score, you get 14 pts instead.

**How is the group leader decided?**  
By the sum of your points on the 6 matches within that World Cup group. It has nothing to do with which group you signed up for — everyone predicts all 72 matches, and the software tracks your per-group totals automatically.
